Chapter I
An Unexpected Party

In a hole in the ground there lived a hobbit. Not a nasty, dirty, wet hole
filled with the ends of worms and an oozy smell.

This hobbit was a very well-to-do hobbit, and his name was Baggins.

Among the dwarves came Fíli and Kíli, youngest of the company.

Chapter V
Riddles in the Dark

Deep down here by the dark water lived old Gollum, a small slimy creature.

"What has it got in its pocketses?" he hissed, and the answer was not
riddles at all, and it crept nearer.

He was talking to himself for want of anyone else to talk to.

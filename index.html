In a hole in the ground there lived a hobbit — and here his adventure can be retold in the cards of the set, exactly as you choose to arrange it. Create your own sections and subsections, then fill them from the set: search by name, type or flavor text, and pick the exact printing — every art is its own card. A card may be placed at many points of the tale; own fewer copies than placements and the extras glow amber, your hunt list for bringing the story to life.
